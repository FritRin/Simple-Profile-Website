Introducing Myself

Welcome to my website, I'm Victoria Shulyak, a third-year university student studying software engineering and cybersecurity. Alongside these subjects I have a passion for web development.

I have always had an interest in technology as I've been interacting with computers ever since I was quite young. When I was in high school, my laptop got a virus when I tried to download something online, and I was fascinated by the behaviour it caused in my laptop. Since then I started to watch videos about viruses to learn more about them.

My interest in programming and web design emerged when I took a class in high school that introduced me to the basics of HTML and CSS. After that I would attempt to design my own website to catalog the books I had read. Wanting to improve my websites, I started to learn coding and have continued to pursue these interest through to tertiary education.
