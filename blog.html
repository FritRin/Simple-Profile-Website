My blog where I talk about my interests and influences in my decision to pursue a career in technology.

danooct1 YouTube Channel

Following my own encounter with a computer virus and beginning my research into them online, I found this channel which demonstrated the effects old and infamous viruses had on the systems they infected. Through seeing the demonstrations and learning about the history and impact of these viruses, my interest in the field of cybersecurity grew and led me into further researching past incidents involving computer viruses and other instances of cybercrime. The very first video I had watched that was published by this channel was one on the CIH virus, a piece of malware that would infect any file that was executed on the infected system and would overwrite the BIOS, removing the operating system on the set date of the 26th of May. A few other topics that were covered included the ILOVEYOU worm and YouAreAnIdiot.org, while all of them targeted old systems, these videos were a great insight into the various methods through which viruses spread.
