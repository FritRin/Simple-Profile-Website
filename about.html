About Me

Biography

I was born in Ukraine and lived there for five years before my family moved to Australia in 2011. Everything here was very different so I had to adapt to the new environment and culture, including learning a new language. My educational journey started with that at Toowoomba Preparatory School, now Toowoomba Anglican School, where I would later have my first exposure to programming and web design.

As was mentioned in my introduction, high school was also when I had my first encounter with computer viruses: the darker side of technology and the internet. Gaming is one of my hobbies and at the time I had really wanted to play a game I had seen on YouTube so I attempted to download it off the internet, only to download a virus instead. At that point in time I had only heard of malware in passing so it was a new and somewhat scary experience. I found myself intrigued by its behaviour and decided to learn more about malware through research online, primarily videos of them being run on virtual machines or old laptops.

Though cybersecurity was not a field I had heard being discussed in 2019, I knew that I wanted to pursue a career that involved dealing with malware and cybercrime. This inspired to start learning programming outside of my classes in school, leading me to learning C++ with videos by CodeCamp and continuing to make websites as a hobby outside of my classes as well.

In 2024 I commenced my studies at UniSQ, studying a bachelor of information and communication technology with a double major in networking and cybersecurity and software engineering. Though my primary interest lies with cybersecurity, I still enjoy programming and creating programs to present a solution to a problem. I am now in my final year of university and through the assignments I have completed, I have found that I greatly value work where I can demonstrate my skills in a practical manner by creating programs or identifying solutions with what I have learnt.
