My Blog

My blog where I talk about my interests and influences in my decision to pursue a career in technology.

danooct1 YouTube Channel

Following my own encounter with a computer virus and beginning my research into them online, I found this channel which demonstrated the effects old and infamous viruses had on the systems they infected. Through seeing the demonstrations and learning about the history and impact of these viruses, my interest in the field of cybersecurity grew and led me into further researching past incidents involving computer viruses and other instances of cybercrime. The very first video I had watched that was published by this channel was one on the CIH virus, a piece of malware that would infect any file that was executed on the infected system and would overwrite the BIOS, removing the operating system on the set date of the 26th of May. A few other topics that were covered included the ILOVEYOU worm and YouAreAnIdiot.org, while all of them targeted old systems, these videos were a great insight into the various methods through which viruses spread.

Our Increasing Reliance on Technology

As technology continues to develop and advance it becomes more present in our lives as it becomes easier to work, shop and fill out forms online. The move to digital has jumped even further in the wake of the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic and it has made it so a lot of our lives and information are now online. In light of such rapid development it is unlikely that regulations can keep up, so I believe it is important to remain aware of what is behind the I also found that I wanted to be a part of the ongoing developments.

Data Breaches

A side effect of the rise in the use of technology and the very quick transition to digital services and storing data digitally was that the protection of consumer data has proven to not be effective. Throughout the 2020s there have been many data breaches of companies that hold sensitive consumer data such as Medibank and Telstra. Reading the articles on these data breaches and learning about how they were accomplished pushed me to start taking extracurricular courses on the website TryHackMe to learn more about cybersecurity tools and the roles and responsibilties of a cybersecurity professional. My focus was primarily on that of a SOC employee where their job is to monitor for any potential attacks and to prevent the possibility of a breach or handle one if the situation arises. Though I have also explored the path of penetration testing where one tests how secure a company's system is, providing insight on how security can be improved.
